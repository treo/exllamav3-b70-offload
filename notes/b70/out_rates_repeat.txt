How to read it: absolute rates repeat to 0.1-2.4% and depend on working-set size (1 GiB here, 1.5 GiB
in the sweep); RATIOS repeat to <=3%, and those are the level at which design conclusions are drawn.
The marginal cost of arithmetic is taken as a difference between two measured kernels, never as a
modelled duty cycle: R6-R2 = -4.4% (Qwen3.8) and -0.1% (GLM) for +47 lane-instructions per 32-lane
word = per 128 B of stream.
