GLM gate geometry and the joint (fusion, split-K) grid that goes with it - fusion is a wash here, and the reason is the shape
============================================================================================================
Geometry measured off the checkpoint (dump_gemv_case.py, layer 3 - GLM's MoE layers start at 3): k=4096,
n=2048, tiles=(256,128), 3.0 MiB per expert per projection at K=3, i.e. 5.1x Qwen3.8's gate bytes and
tiles_n = 128 vs 40. Consequence for the dispatch rule: a SEPARATE gate launch over 10 experts already puts
128 x 2 x 10 = 2,560 threads on a 2,048-slot device, so D8's fill condition - the reason fusing pays
1.13-1.85x on Qwen3.8 - never fires for GLM. This is why 'not evaluated - no measured gate geometry' was
worth closing: the plan prescribes two cards for exactly this model.

Cells: 2 E x 3 rows x {sep,fused} x KS 1/2/4; anchor 19.0-19.2 us in every cell; every cell bit-exact.

READING IT: sep wins 4 of 6 cells and the two 'fused' wins are 3-7%, i.e. inside/near the tie band, so the
GLM dispatch is: do not fuse gate+up; run them as two launches with KS=2 (rows>=16) or KS=4 (rows<=8).
Absolute costs are 5x Qwen's per expert, matching bytes rather than weights - at this shape the card is
closer to its DRAM limit, which is also why extra split-K stops paying past KS=4.
