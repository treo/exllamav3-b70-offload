Two of the three traces are usable. q268 FAILED its validity gate (top-28 of its first window shares 0/28 experts
with its last, against 1.5 expected from two random subsets of 512) — an index-basis change mid-trace, not a
routing finding, so everything about q268 below and above is excluded from the conclusion (LESSONS 89). Its
AGGREGATE distribution is identical to q205's (top-28 pick share 0.115 vs 0.112), which is what proved the
time axis, not the marginal, was broken.

And the churn metric that started this investigation is partly an artefact of sample size — adjacency jaccard at
k=84 rises monotonically with the window: Qwen 0.402 (16 tok) -> 0.474 (32) -> 0.545 (64) -> 0.688 (128);
GLM 0.425 -> 0.454 -> 0.484. Reading only the 32-token number would have said "half the hot set turns over every
half minute", which the longer windows do not support.

VERDICT — D16 confirmed, and its reopening condition did not fire. The hot set's core is durable (survival ~90 %
over 400-500 tokens) and refreshing the tier buys almost nothing: 4.9 points of picks on Qwen at card-like
capacity, 1.5 points on GLM, 0.3 points at k>=128. Promotion machinery that moves experts every interval would be
paying transfer (0.09 ms/expert at Qwen sizes, 0.66 ms at GLM sizes — D20) for a gain measured in fractions of a
percent of picks. The right shape is therefore:

  * a STATIC tier sized by pick mass, not by rank;
  * rank-dependent hysteresis — freeze the core, let the boundary move, because that is where the measured
    turnover actually is (55 % vs 94 % survival on Qwen, 27 % vs 90 % on GLM);
  * and accept the residue: a frozen top-84 still misses 25 % of Qwen picks and a refreshed one only reduces that
    to 20 %. That 20 % is not a policy failure — top-84-of-512 cannot serve a tail — so it is a CAPACITY
    shortfall, which is precisely what the card is for and what `-mcpa` structurally cannot fix.

What this does NOT cover: single decode traces of 384-512 tokens. Whether the hot set persists ACROSS sessions,
topics, or a whole conversation (the horizon a static tier really needs) is unmeasured, and GLM's 18/42 halving
rate is censored by its short trace. A 4k-token multi-session trace is the cheap follow-up — the instrument now
supports it, including the validity gate that would have caught this class of error before a conclusion did.
