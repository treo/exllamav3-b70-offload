Why the sizes: plan_parent_budget.py's capacity bill. The 2 GiB in the plan was a guess that treated the
3090 as mostly full of KV. These checkpoints are hybrid linear-attention with compressed KV:
  Qwen3.8  36/48 layers linear, 12 full with 2 KV heads -> 24 KiB KV/token, 108 MiB state/sequence
  GLM      34/45 linear, 11 MLA-sparse with kv_lora_rank 512 -> 22 KiB/token, 136 MiB state/sequence
and the big tables that would have eaten the card - 49.91 GiB of per-layer n-gram embeddings plus
embed/lm_head on Qwen3.8 - are NOT resident: modules/row_table.py row-gathers them from host/disk
(ngram_gather_cpu). What actually has to be in VRAM is 4.05 / 5.67 GiB of streamed non-expert weights,
leaving 16.6 GiB (Qwen3.8, 4k ctx, b=1) and 16.6 GiB (GLM) for experts.
Effect, from the rows above:
  Qwen3.8@2.68  h 97.6% -> 100.0%, CPU residue 0.42 -> 0.00 ms/token  (all 512 experts per layer homed)
  GLM@3.05      h 70.8% -> 81.1%, CPU residue 19.6 -> 12.6 ms/token  (91 -> 130 of 288 per layer)
Both effects are larger than anything the hot/cold ordering choices produce, and the slice should be
computed at load from the config (KV/token x ctx x rows + state x rows + streamed non-expert + reserve),
not set to a constant.
