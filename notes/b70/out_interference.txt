Conclusion, stated with its scope: a saturated B70 doing device-local work neither slows the parent nor is slowed
by it. This is the first measurement supporting the zero-interference assumption that the max(device terms)
balance in D14/D18 has made all week - and the assumption is still UNTESTED where it is most likely to fail:
the card moving activations over the link while the parent streams expert weights from host DRAM (measured
42.6 GiB/s host read, 13.4 GiB/s pinned link, and `out_prefill_decomp.txt` shows copy already at 39 % of the
prefill floor). That pairing contends for the host memory controller and the root complex; this experiment did
not touch either. Until it is measured, "no interference" applies only to resident-weight, device-local work.
Method note for future A/B work on this box: interleave, never block. Blocking A-then-B measures warm state.
