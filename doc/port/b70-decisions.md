## What this ledger deliberately does NOT decide

- **Whether the plan's headline prefill multiplier is real.** 1.69× (or 1.34× over `-mcpa`) is a *device-term*
  result resting on the 1988 ms intercept being reducible. `run_plan_close.sh` S1 measures that intercept by
  kernel; until it prints, this stays a projection **[P]** and no row above should be quoted as throughput.
- **Static vs dynamic tiering.** Blocked on hot-set dwell time, which the stored live profiles cannot answer —
  they hold aggregated per-layer counts, not a time series. Needs a tracer change (`--dwell`), not a new probe.
- **Portability beyond the two families measured.** Everything here is Qwen3.8-shaped (E=512, topk 10, K=2..6)
  plus GLM (E=288, topk 8 + 1 dense shared expert, K=3, `tiles_n`=128 ⇒ do not fuse). Two more facts came out of
  the GLM audit that the plan has not yet absorbed: shared experts are a **dense `GatedMLP`**, so they must NOT
  be charged the `ceil(rows/16)` bucket law (an early version of this doc did, producing ~1.5 s/chunk of fiction),
  and every GLM token pays **8 routed + 1 shared ≈ +12.5 %** per-token expert work.
- **Grouped routing as a placement unit.** `block_sparse_mlp.py` carries `n_group`/`topk_group` (DeepSeek-style
  group-limited top-k, "the grouped DS3 router (n_group > 1, Ling 3.x)"). A router that restricts a token to
  `topk_group` groups structurally bounds how many placements a token can touch — potentially a better locality
  lever than any cache policy here — and it is unexamined. First model that ships `n_group > 1` forces this.

## Two questions that are the operator's, not mine

1. **Is sustained ~87 W on the B70 acceptable in the target chassis?** The soak says the card holds the anchor
   duty cycle at ~87 W with no throttling, which is *legal* but is a thermals/dust/noise decision for the machine
   it will live in, and the plan's power budget assumed nothing.
2. **Does the deployment actually run speculative decoding?** D22 prices the verify batch and the engine supports
   the path, but if the target configuration never drafts, that whole thread ships dark and the near-term prize
   is only the prefill/expert-tier one.
