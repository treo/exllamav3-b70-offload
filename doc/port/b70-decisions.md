## What this ledger deliberately does NOT decide

- **Whether the plan's headline prefill multiplier is real.** 1.69× (or 1.34× over `-mcpa`) is a *device-term*
  result resting on the 1988 ms intercept being reducible — and `out_prefill_decomp.txt` has now decomposed it:
  real model work is ~1.15 s per 4096-token chunk, and the remainder of that "floor" is the offload path moving
  activations (D24). So the multiplier is probably *conservative*, but its sign now hinges on an unmeasured
  quantity — the card's activation path — which is why it stays a projection **[P]** rather than a promise.
- **Static vs dynamic tiering — DECIDED** (D16 above; `notes/b70/out_dwell.txt`). Listed here only to record what
  closed it: two valid traces (Qwen 512 tok, GLM 384 tok) that pass a first-vs-last validity gate, adaptation
  worth 4.9 and 1.5 points of picks. What remains open is the HORIZON — whether the hot set persists across
  sessions and topics, which is the range a static tier must actually survive. The instrument supports that
  measurement now; the measurement does not exist.
- **Portability beyond the two families measured.** Everything here is Qwen3.8-shaped (E=512, topk 10, K=2..6)
  plus GLM (E=288, topk 8 + 1 dense shared expert, K=3, `tiles_n`=128 ⇒ do not fuse). Two more facts came out of
  the GLM audit that the plan has not yet absorbed: shared experts are a **dense `GatedMLP`**, so they must NOT
  be charged the `ceil(rows/16)` bucket law (an early version of this doc did, producing ~1.5 s/chunk of fiction),
  and every GLM token pays **8 routed + 1 shared ≈ +12.5 %** per-token expert work.
- **Grouped routing as a placement unit.** `block_sparse_mlp.py` carries `n_group`/`topk_group` (DeepSeek-style
  group-limited top-k, "the grouped DS3 router (n_group > 1, Ling 3.x)"). A router that restricts a token to
  `topk_group` groups structurally bounds how many placements a token can touch — potentially a better locality
  lever than any cache policy here — and it is unexamined. First model that ships `n_group > 1` forces this.

## Two questions that are the operator's, not mine

1. **Is sustained ~87 W on the B70 acceptable in the target chassis?** The soak says the card holds the anchor
   duty cycle at ~87 W with no throttling, which is *legal* but is a thermals/dust/noise decision for the machine
   it will live in, and the plan's power budget assumed nothing.
2. **Does the deployment actually run speculative decoding?** — *partly answered from the artifacts, and it
   flips D22's scope.* The engine supports the path (`mtp_draft`/`dflash*` caps, per-component draft worker), and
   **GLM-5.3 has the hardware for it in the checkpoint** (MTP head at layer 45, matching arch module). The
   **Qwen3.8 exports do not** — no MTP layer in config, no MTP tensors in any shard — so the M = 4–16 pricing,
   which was computed from **Qwen live routing**, currently applies to the model where it is *available* (GLM)
   rather than the model where it was *measured* (Qwen). Either re-price the verify batch on GLM routing, or find
   out whether the deployment intends ngram/dflash drafting on Qwen; until one of those holds, treat the verify
   win as GLM-scoped. Note drafting also pays a **43rd MoE layer** (the MTP head routes to 288 experts of its
   own), so draft cost is not free at GLM's expert size, and the index file for this export is stale in a way
   that hid all of this from me at first (LESSONS 86).

## Plan-phase status at close-out

**Closed by measurement since the decode chapter** (each with its artifact beside it):

- the prefill floor, decomposed: ~1.15 s of real model work per 4096-token chunk, with 39–81 % of what had been
  called "non-expert" being the tiering mechanism's own copy traffic (D24, `out_prefill_decomp.txt`);
- D17's operating point, which its own rule could not decide: co-scheduling worth 5–6 % at E=128/256
  (`out_probe_gemm_midE.txt`), replacing an invented 10× L2 bracket with a measured ~4×;
- bucket cost against quant, three rungs, `4.8 + 1.19·K` µs (`out_prefill_kscale.txt`);
- hot-set dwell: D16 confirmed, adaptation worth 1.5–4.9 points of picks, core survival ~90 % against ~27–55 % at
  the boundary, and the ~20 % residue identified as **capacity** rather than policy (`out_dwell.txt`);
- parent ⇄ card interference: nil for device-resident card work, with the scope stated rather than assumed (D25,
  `out_interference.txt`);
- GLM: parent leg (101.1 µs/layer, 71 % stall), prefill (126 → 167 tok/s under `-mcpa`, 80.6 % copy), inventory
  from shard headers, and the promotion sign shown to be **per-phase** (D19/D20, `out_glm_prefill_mcpa.txt`);
- verify batching scoped to the checkpoints that actually ship a draft head (D22 amended: GLM yes, Qwen3.8 no);
- the planners wired to the decisions (D14's crossing point in `policy()`, `slab_advice` rungs) and the memory
  guard moved into the loader path where a forgotten queue script cannot bypass it;
- and this ledger written down at all — D14–D25 lived only in conversation until now, which is how a decision
  record stops being a contract.

**What the implementation phase inherits, and why it cannot be measured from here:**

1. **The activation path (D24)** — the only remaining measurement that can move the headline number, and it needs
   a card-side staged buffer plus link instrumentation, i.e. code, not a probe.
2. **The vertical slice** (`b70-slice.md`) — closes D19's stall term and is the first test of composition; needs
   the handoff hook described in `b70-architecture.md` §13.
3. Three cheap measurements deliberately left undone, each with the decision it would sharpen: K=3 bucket cost in
   the probe (so the `4.8 + 1.19·K` fit is not interpolated across a gap), GLM-geometry bucket cost (so the GLM
   rows stop borrowing Qwen rates), and a multi-session dwell trace (so the static tier is sized against a real
   horizon rather than a 512-token one).
4. Horizons no trace reaches: `-mcpa` churn over a day of traffic, and whether a promoted set trained on one
   conversation transfers to the next.
